About Us

At Sunrise Kitchen, we bring the bold and vibrant flavors of Haiti straight to the heart of Indianapolis. Our Griot Platter, served with perfectly crispy fried plantains and a side of tangy, spicy pikliz, is a customer favorite—and once you try it, you'll understand why! But for us, it’s about more than just great food. Sunrise Kitchen is a place where culture, community, and cuisine come together.

We take pride in sharing the warmth of Haitian hospitality with every dish we serve. From our rich, slow-cooked stews to our sizzling, golden-fried delicacies, every bite tells a story of tradition, passion, and home. Serving our Indianapolis community is an honor, and being able to represent Haiti through our food makes it even more special. Whether you're trying Haitian cuisine for the first time or looking for a taste of home, we welcome you with open arms and a plate full of love.


Come experience the flavors of Haiti, right here at Sunrise Kitchen!
